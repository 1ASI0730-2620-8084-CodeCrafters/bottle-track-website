2. Description of the service

BottleTrack is a web platform for the management and tracking of bottled beverage transport. It allows the user to follow the progress of a delivery route, to consult the data reported by the internet of things devices installed on the vehicle and the cargo, and to report transport incidents with supporting evidence.

The information reported by the devices depends on the sensors available in each vehicle and on the connectivity conditions of the route. CodeCrafters does not guarantee an uninterrupted supply of that data.
