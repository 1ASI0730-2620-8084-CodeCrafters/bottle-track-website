Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how CodeCrafters processes personal data when you visit the BottleTrack website or use the BottleTrack platform, in line with the Peruvian Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733) and its regulation.

1. Who is responsible

CodeCrafters, the team that develops and operates BottleTrack, is responsible for the processing of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us through the channels published on the home page.

2. Data we collect

On the website: the name, company, segment and message that you enter in the contact form.

In the platform: the data of the user accounts (name, email and role) and the operational data of the transport, such as vehicle location, cargo temperature, impact records, delivery results and the evidence attached to an incident, which may include personal information of drivers and operators.

3. Purpose of the processing

We use the data only to answer your contact request, to provide the tracking, monitoring and incident reporting features of BottleTrack, and to keep the platform secure. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Consent

By sending the contact form or by using the platform, you give your consent to the processing described here. You can withdraw it at any time through the contact channels of the home page.

5. Security

CodeCrafters applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data against loss, unauthorized access and alteration, including access by role and the integrity of incident records.

6. Sharing of data

The operational data belongs to the contracting organization and is visible only to the users of that organization. We share data with third parties only when it is necessary to provide the service, such as the hosting and map services, or when the law requires it.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, cancel and object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, write to the CodeCrafters team through the contact channels of the home page.

8. Retention

We keep the data only for the time needed to fulfill the purposes described above or the obligations of the contracting organization, and then we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. Information stored in your browser

The website stores only your language preference in the local storage of your browser, so that the page opens in the language you chose. It does not use advertising cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

CodeCrafters may update this policy to reflect changes in the platform or in the applicable regulation. The date of the last update appears at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be addressed to the CodeCrafters team through the contact channels published on the home page.
